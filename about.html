About Blue Gems and Jewels

Nearly Three Decades of Retailer Trust

A Mumbai-based fine jewellery manufacturing company built on one simple understanding: every piece we make eventually carries the retailer's name, reputation, and relationship with their customer.

Our Story

A Manufacturing Partner, Not a Distant Supplier

Est. 1996Mumbai, India

Since 1996, Blue Gems and Jewels has worked with independent jewellery retailers across India, creating fine jewellery that brings together craftsmanship, diamond quality, modern manufacturing, and retailer-focused service.

We are not only manufacturers of jewellery. We are manufacturing partners for retailers who need confidence in the product, confidence in the process, and confidence in the people behind the work — from the first design discussion to the final dispatch.

Vision

To be the manufacturing partner India's jewellery retailers trust without question — held to a standard of craftsmanship and compliance that stands confidently anywhere in the world.

Mission

To manufacture jewellery that helps Indian retailers sell with confidence, upheld by certified, disciplined standards built to serve any market we choose to enter.
