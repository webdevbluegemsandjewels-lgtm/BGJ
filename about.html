Our Story

Manufacturing partners, not distant suppliers.

Est. 1996Mumbai, India

Since 1996, Blue Gems and Jewels has worked with independent jewellery retailers across India, creating fine jewellery that brings together craftsmanship, diamond quality, modern manufacturing, and retailer-focused service.

We are not only manufacturers of jewellery. We are manufacturing partners for retailers who need confidence in the product, confidence in the process, and confidence in the people behind the work — from the first design discussion to the final dispatch.
