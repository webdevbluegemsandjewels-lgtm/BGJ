The Manufacturing Journey

From requirement to showcase-ready.

01
Understanding Requirement

Listening First

Design direction, karat, finish, product category, customer preference, timeline, and commercial purpose are all understood before production moves forward — so the jewellery works for the retailer's customer, showcase, and market.

02
Design Review

A Manufacturing Point of View

We review proportion, comfort, durability, stone placement, setting strength, weight balance, and ease of wear — refining anything that could affect strength, comfort, or production practicality.

03
Material Planning

18K, 14K & 9K Gold

Yellow, white, and rose finishes each behave differently during production, polishing, and setting. Our team plans the manufacturing process around each karat's specific behaviour.

04
Production

Where Karigar Skill Becomes Central

Machines support the process, but final quality depends on trained hands and careful judgement. Every curve, edge, joint, setting area, and surface is shaped with attention.

05
Diamond Setting

Precision at Every Angle

Diamonds are selected, aligned, set, and secured to support brilliance, balance, and durability. Spacing must feel clean; the finish around the setting must be neat from every angle.

06
Finishing

Where Craftsmanship Becomes Visible

Surfaces, edges, polish, setting areas, joints, and closures are checked closely. A well-made piece can lose its impact if the final finish is not clean.

07
Quality Control

Not a Final Formality

Reviewed for strength, accuracy, comfort, setting security, finish, alignment, proportion, and presentation at every stage — corrected before delivery, never after.

08
Documentation

Hallmarking & Clarity

As a BIS hallmark registered and GJEPC member company, documentation is treated as an essential part of professional manufacturing — not a separate formality.

09
Delivery

Protecting the Relationship

Before dispatch, every piece is reviewed as a complete, finished product — ready to carry the retailer's name and confidence into the showcase.

"Because our manufacturing is handled in-house at our Andheri East facility, we know where the piece is made, who is working on it, and whether it needs correction before moving ahead."
